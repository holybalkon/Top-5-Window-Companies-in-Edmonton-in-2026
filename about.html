Top 5 Window Companies in Edmonton in 2026

This page explains how the Top 5 Window Companies in Edmonton guide is compiled, what evidence it relies on and where our commercial interest lies.

What this ranking is

This is an editorial selection, not an independent consumer survey. Rankings reflect the stated editorial criteria and do not represent official Google, Yelp or ENERGY STAR rankings.

Scoring factors

Companies are considered against five factors of equal weight (20% each), using the same evidence standard for all five:

  1. Independently checkable product quality
  2. Installed value
  3. Warranty scope
  4. Customer-review evidence
  5. Service accessibility

We show an editorial position rather than a numeric score. A numeric mark would need enough dated evidence in every category, which this edition does not claim to have.

Evidence and verification rules

  • Claims taken from a company's own website are tagged company-stated.
  • Google ratings and review counts are shown separately from our ordering and are only published with a source and a date. This edition does not include them.
  • We do not invent review excerpts, review counts, response times or prices.
  • Warranty documents, certifications and promotions should be rechecked before you sign a contract.
  • Where public evidence is thinner, the card says so.
