Window replacement questions and answers

Frame material, window count, triple- versus double-pane glazing, size, custom shapes, structural repairs, trim work and whether the job is a retrofit or full-frame replacement all change the final amount.

Ask for window count, model, glazing, labour, disposal, taxes and warranty terms. Every company should quote the same configuration so the totals can be compared.

Compare U-factor, Energy Rating and air leakage for the exact window model. Also ask about low-E coating and gas fill, and check certifications by model rather than by brand.
